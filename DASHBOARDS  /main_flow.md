# 🏦 SBI Saathi
### AI-Powered Customer Outreach & Financial Inclusion

SBI Saathi is an **Agentic AI platform** designed to proactively identify eligible SBI customers, understand their needs, communicate in their preferred language, verify documents, coordinate Banking Mitras, and complete eligible banking services with minimal branch dependency.

The platform combines **AI agents, automated workflows, multilingual communication, document intelligence, and human-in-the-loop verification** to create a personalized end-to-end banking journey.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Customers often miss banking benefits because of:

- Dormant or inactive accounts
- Incomplete KYC
- Lack of awareness about eligible schemes
- Language and digital-literacy barriers
- Difficulty understanding required documents
- Unnecessary branch visits
- Limited follow-up after initial outreach

SBI Saathi addresses these gaps through proactive and personalized AI-driven engagement.

---

## 💡 Solution

SBI Saathi continuously analyzes eligible customer signals and coordinates multiple AI agents to:

- Identify eligible customers
- Select the appropriate outreach channel
- Communicate in regional languages
- Capture customer interest and consent
- Request and verify documents
- Identify missing or incorrect documents
- Recommend additional eligible benefits
- Decide between online and offline processing
- Coordinate with Banking Mitras
- Schedule appointments
- Track service completion
- Collect customer feedback

---

## 🤖 Agentic AI Model

### Saathi Swagat
Handles customer onboarding, KYC completion and initial engagement.

### Saathi Disha
Identifies relevant schemes, benefits and financial services for customers.

### Saathi Bhavishya
Focuses on long-term financial needs such as insurance, pension and investment opportunities.

These agents work together through automated workflows to manage the customer's journey from **identification to service completion**.

### Journey Stages

| # | Stage | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Customer Identification | AI identifies eligible customers using banking signals and eligibility criteria. |
| 02 | Personalized Outreach | Customer is contacted through WhatsApp, SMS or Voice. |
| 03 | Interest Capture | Customer responds and provides consent to proceed. |
| 04 | Document Request | AI provides the required documents and instructions. |
| 05 | Document Submission | Customer securely submits the required documents. |
| 06 | Verification & Validation | AI and banking systems verify documents and detect issues. |
| 07 | KYC Completion | Required KYC/eKYC processes are completed. |
| 08 | Offer Activation | Eligible schemes, products and benefits are activated. |
| 09 | Confirmation | Customer receives status and service confirmation. |
| 10 | Support & Relationship | AI continues support and customer engagement. |
